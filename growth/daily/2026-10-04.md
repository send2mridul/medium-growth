### 2. The Harness Is the Product - How Agents Are Actually Built in 2026

**Author** - Andrii Tkachuk

**Published** - 1 day ago

**Article** - [Read on Medium](https://medium.com/@andrii.tkachuk7/the-harness-is-the-product-how-agents-are-actually-built-in-2026-4b751480f60c)

**Why engage** - The article offers a detailed production-agent architecture and already has active discussion. Its distinction between model-requested clarification and harness-enforced approval creates room for a concrete recovery caveat.

Ready-to-post comment

> The sharpest distinction here is between model-initiated clarification and policy-enforced approval. I would push it one step further and make every pending action replay-safe. If a durable task resumes after a timeout, duplicate webhook, or worker crash, the approval should bind to an immutable action digest and execution should be idempotent. Otherwise the harness can enforce the right human decision twice. Controlled capability needs recovery semantics as much as policy.
