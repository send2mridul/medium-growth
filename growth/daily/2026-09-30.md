> The distinction between contribution throughput and maintainer formation is useful here. A patch can be correct and still contribute almost nothing to shared understanding. I wonder if the workable middle ground is to review AI-assisted contributions partly on knowledge transfer: clear provenance, an explanation of the design choice, tests, and a contributor who can answer follow-up questions.
